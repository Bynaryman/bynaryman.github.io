## A little history

Rodec is a Belgian analogue-audio manufacturer with [roots going back to 1954](https://www.rodec.com/about-us). Its [2010 catalogue, page 7](http://www.rodec.de/rodec2010.pdf#page=7) traces the mixer family from the MX18 in 1986 to the MX180 in 1990, then the MKIII in 1999.

The MKIII has four main input channels and a dedicated DJ microphone channel. Each channel has its own gain, three-band EQ, and fader; the main channels also have balance controls. The [operating manual](https://www.liveline.be/wa_files/RODEC_20manual_MX180MKIII.pdf) documents the signal routing and controls.

## Inside the mixer

I bought replacement faders as part of this maintenance. The internal photographs show the fader assemblies behind the front panel, alongside the upright channel boards and their ribbon cables. A Bourns fader is visible, with separate wires where the neighbouring assemblies use ribbon cable.
