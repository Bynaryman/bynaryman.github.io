[604 reads as GOA](https://trancentral.tv/2016/03/zion604-special-new-compilation-an-interview-with-label-boss-shidapu/): 6 for G, 0 for O, and 4 for A. For Goa Day, I put both on the oscilloscope.

In X–Y mode, one input controls the horizontal position of the trace and the other controls its vertical position. The two signals draw a trajectory across the screen, forming the numbers and letters.
